Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need Travel Insurance?

Travellers do need an Indian Visa and Travel Insurance to enter the country, apart from a valid Passport and a ticket for return/onward journey. Electronic Visas come in handy for short recreational visits as well. Do check out the Visa options and apply accordingly.

How can we commute locally in different places

Flights, Trains, Luxury Coaches, and Best-in-Class Rental Cars make it easy to explore India comfortably and safely. Public transport, though efficient and relatively cheap, is often quite crowded and not advisable.

What should I pack?

It all depends on your destination and season of travel. Much of India has a patchy climate: warmer in the days and cooler in the evenings. A light jacket or layering is best to accommodate the varying temperatures. Also, we suggest shoes that are comfortable for walking, including sandals and tennis shoes. A head cover may be required for women and men at certain sacred sites and places of worship.

Is there any Dress code?

Dressing moderately often helps avoid attracting unwanted attention, even if dress codes are a tad liberal in the cities. Make sure to respect sentiments at places that do impose strict dress codes.

Are vaccinations required?

Immunizations are not required to visit India. (Exception: if you are traveling from an area infected with Yellow Fever, you must have a certificate.) Depending on the season and region of travel, certain vaccinations and/ or medications are suggested and we will recommend preventative measures. We do encourage all travelers to be current on routine immunizations. Also, we recommend the Tetanus and Hepatitis A vaccine.
