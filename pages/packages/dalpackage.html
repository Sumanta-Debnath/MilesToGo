Travel Itinerary

Day 1

Meet our representative upon arrival at Dalhousie and transfer to your pre-booked hotel. Check in the hotel and after some time resting in the room will leave for Panchpula which is a gorgeous site, just 2 miles away. Besides this, travelers also get an opportunity to explore the other beautiful appeals of this place which are Subhash Baoli and Laxmi Narayan Shrine. Night stay at hotel.

Day 2

After breakfast, head off to Khajjiar where you get to explore many attractions like Khajji Naga Temple, Kalatop wildlife sanctuary and many more. Later proceed to Chamba and after exploring the sights, get back to Dalhousie. Dinner and night stay at hotel.

Day 3

Checkout from the hotel for your onward destination.
