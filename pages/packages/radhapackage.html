Travel Itinerary

Day 1

On arrival at Port Blair in the morning, our representative will be there to greet you and transfer you to the hotel. After check-in at the hotel, we will proceed for a Port Blair City Tour that includes the Fisheries Museum (Aquarium) - exhibits species of marine life peculiar to the islands, Anthropological Museum - displays tribal essential and artefacts'. Later, we will be visiting the National Monument - the Cellular Jail and see the saga of the Indian Freedom struggle through the Light and Sound Show that take place within the complex itself. Return back to the hotel for an overnight stay at Port Blair.

Day 2

After breakfast, we will take you to Jetty to reach Havelock Island through ferry ride as per its timings. On reaching at Havelock Island, our representative will be there to receive you and assist you to check-in at your pre-booked hotel / resort. After check-in at the hotel/resort, we will visit the Radhanagar Beach, one of the most pleasing beaches on the Islands that has been ranked 7th out of the most beautiful beaches of Asia by the TIME magazine. Later, we will come back to the hotel / resort for an overnight stay at Havelock Island.

Day 3

After breakfast, we will leave to visit the Elephant beach in a sharing fibre speedboat that will take you around 40 min to reach the beach. Elephant beach provides an ideal platform to starter and professional snorkellers seeking for peaceful blue shallow water. Further, it also offers an ideal place to beach lovers who like to spend some leisure time in the beach and make the best of their vacation. Well surrounded with the beautiful white sand and fallen trees, the beach is counted among one of the most pictorial beaches on the island. Later, return back to the hotel for an overnight stay in the hotel/resort.

Day 4

Morning is free for leisure. Later in the afternoon, we will take you to Havelock Jetty as per ferry timing to commute to Port Blair through ferry. On return from Havelock Island, we will transfer you to the hotel for an overnight stay at Port Blair.

Day 5

Checkout from the hotel for your onward destination.
