Travel Itinerary

Day 1

On arrival at Guwahati in the morning, you will be transfered to your pre-booked hotel in Kaziranga. In the afternoon post lunch, enjoy jeep safari in the Western Range (Bagori)/ Central Range (Kahora) of Kaziranga National Park. Evening: Free at relaxation. Overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 2

In the early morning hours, enjoy an elephant safari at Central Range (Kohora)/ Western Range (Bagori) of Kaziranga National Park. In the late morning hours, visit Kaziranga Orchid Park to observe more than 500 varieties of wild orchids found all in North-East Indian states, 132 species of sour fruits and leafy vegetables, 12 species of cane, 46 species of bamboo and a wide variety of local fishes. Rest of the day visit the beautiful tea gardens bordering the Kaziranga National Park. Overnight stay at hotel.

Day 3

Checkout from the hotel for your onward destination.
