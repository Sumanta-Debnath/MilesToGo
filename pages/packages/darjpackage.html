Travel Itinerary

Day 1

Meet our representative upon arrival at Darjeeling and transfer to your pre-booked hotel. The hill journey to Darjeeling will pass through numerous green tea gardens that provide a breathtaking sight of the place. On reaching, transfer to a hotel. Overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 2

Get up early to sunrise from the Tiger Hill. We will be leaving the hotel around 04.00 am for covering the 10 kms distance to reach the Tiger Hill. Upon reaching enjoy the panoramic views of sunrise from Mount Everest along with Kangchenjunga from the observation deck. Return to Darjeeling via Batasia Loop. Upon arrival, transfer to the hotel, fresh-n-up and enjoy tasty breakfast.

Day 3

After breakfast, we will proceed for an excursion to Mirik Town. The town of Mirik is nestled in forest hills at an altitude of 5800 ft. The Sumendu Lake is the main attraction of Mirik. It is surrounded by beautiful pine trees, orange orchards and tea estates. Enjoy boating in the lake and admire the beauty of nature. One can also stroll through the Pasupati Market. Later, return to the hotel in Darjeeling. In the evening you are free to stroll through the mall. Overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 4

In the morning after having breakfast, checkout from the hotel for your onward destination.
