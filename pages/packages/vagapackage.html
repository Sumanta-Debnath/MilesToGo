Travel Itinerary

Day 1

On arrival at Panaji in the morning, you will be transfered to your pre-booked hotel. Rest of the day free for self activities. Overnight stay at hotel.

Day 2

After breakfast spend full day to explore the North Goa. Visit Lord Bodgeshwar Temple, Vagator Beach, Anjuna Beach, Fort Aguada, Calangute Beach, Baga Beach etc. In the evening, you will return to hotel for overnight.

Day 3

After breakfast explore South Goa. Visit Miramar Beach, Mangeshi Temple, Shanta Durga Temple, Margao, Colva Beach, evening boat cruise. Overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 4

After breakfast you will be free to enjoy the natural beauty of Goa. This day is a day of relaxation. Enjoy the sun, sand, and the sea at beaches at own in Goa. Here you can have some of the finest local drinks and other delicacies. The night is spent at the hotel.

Day 5

Checkout from the hotel for your onward destination.
